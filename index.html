BOSTON

WELCOME TO THE CITY

Modern Boston invites you to wander cobblestone streets steeped in revolutionary lore before diving headfirst into a thriving, witty hub of innovation and culture.
Grab a lobster roll and explore everything from the high-tech startups of the Seaport to historic Faneuil Hall Marketplace, where merchants have hawked wares since 1742. You can follow the iconic Freedom Trail past the Old North Church in the morning and catch a cutting-edge show in the Theater District by evening;
there’s an adventure waiting everywhere you turn, bridging centuries with every step

MUSEUM OF FINE ARTS

Discover a world of contemporary art - enriched in history, culture, and a multitude of artistic design.

Location: 465 Huntington Ave, Boston, MA 02115

ISABELLA STEWART GARDNER MUSEUM

Founded by Isabella Stewart Gardner in 1903. A home turned into a museum full of European, Asian and American art from over centuries.

Location: 25 Evans Way, Boston, MA, 02115

MUSEUM OF ILLUSIONS

Discover a world full of surprises and illusions at this mind-blowing attraction. Stand on ceilings or stare into a hypnosis wheel. All fun in this house.

Location: 200 State St #2, Boston, MA 02109

DOWNTOWN BOSTON

Explore restaurants, shopping, bars, and more in Bostons Historic Downtown area.

CHINA TOWN

Hot Pot ? Dim Sum ? Boba ? Discover the taste of authentic chinese cuisine in this hotspot location.

NORTH END

All roads lead to rome - or atleast here in boston! Explore the authentic flavor of Italy right here in Boston. AKA little Italy.

FENWAY PARK

Home to the Boston Red Sox, Fenway Park is one of the most historic and loved ballparks in all of Major League Baseball. A visit here offers an authentic experience of baseballs history and a memorable experience for any sports fanatic.

FANUEIL HALL MARKET PLACE

Home to historical figures of the likes of Samuel Adams, Faneuil hall is features Quincy Market, a bustling food hall with numerous shops and restaurants. Experience a vibrant atmosphere with a mix of history, shopping, dining, and street performers.

Location: 1 Faneuil Hall Square, Boston, MA 02109

BOSTON TEA PARTY SHIP & MUSEUM

Travel back in time to 1773 and come aboard an authentically restored replica ship of the Boston Tea Party! Participate in the re-enactment of the historical event by throwing tea overboard into the Harbor, along with witnessing the only known surviving tea chest from the original event.

Location: 306 Congress Street Boston, MA 02210

TD GARDEN

Home of the Boston Celtics - The TD Garden offers many live events; From watching All star players like Jaylen Brown, to going to see you favorite artist in concert, this mega venue checks all the boxes of a perfect saturday night on the town.

Location: 100 Legends Way, Boston, MA 02114

SAM ADAMS BREWERY

Come have a pint at this iconic brewery! Offering a range of unique, classic New England favorites, learn about the brewing process, participate in tastings and enjoy spaces where new beers are created.

Location: 30 Germania St, Boston, MA 02130

HOUSE OF BLUES

Immerse yourself in the performances of all your favorite artist! Located next to Fenway Park, the house of blues has been the go-to spot for some of your favorite artists on the road tours.

Location: 15 Lansdowne St, Boston, MA 02215
